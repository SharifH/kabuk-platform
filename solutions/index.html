Controls and transparency

Questions hotels ask.

Is there a setup fee for hotels?

No. There is no setup fee for hotels to onboard with KabuK Platform. Commercial terms for individual channels are reviewed with you during onboarding.

Can I choose which channels sell my rooms?

Channel eligibility and controls depend on your agreement, the supported integration, and distribution settings. We review available options during onboarding.

Will I see the final price shown by every reseller?

Not yet. Rate transparency is planned. When it is released, visibility will depend on accessible channel data and downstream distribution arrangements, so it may not cover every reseller.

Will I automatically appear on HafH, Omakastay, and all KabuK Exchange partners?

A single onboarding experience is designed to simplify access, but channel activation depends on technical readiness, eligibility, commercial terms, and approval.

Can I compare rates with nearby hotels?

Comparable-property insights are part of the planned experience; availability and coverage will be communicated when released.

Are white-label tools free?

Some white-label packages are planned at no cost and others at low cost. Pricing, scope, and eligibility will be announced when confirmed. Contact us to discuss your requirements.
