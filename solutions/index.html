Solutions

One foundation, shaped to different travel business models.

Hotels, destinations, suppliers, and travel companies coordinate the same core workflows in different ways. We scope each engagement to the business—not the other way around.

Hotels & Hotel Groups

Connect your properties to the systems behind distribution.

KabuK Platform is designed to support accommodation connectivity, reservation workflows, pricing controls, and the operational processes needed to participate in connected travel commerce. Distribution through KabuK Exchange is a separate product pathway.

Typical topics to discuss

  • Property and room content
  • Rate and availability controls
  • Reservation lifecycle
  • Connectivity through existing PMS or channel manager

Discuss Hotel Connectivity

Tourism Boards & Destinations

Build a more connected destination ecosystem.

Explore how common technology services could support regional accommodation connectivity, inventory coordination, and access to distribution channels. Solutions should be scoped to the needs of each destination and participating businesses.

Typical topics to discuss

  • Regional accommodation connectivity
  • Inventory coordination
  • Participation models for local businesses
  • Pathways to distribution channels

Explore Destination Solutions

Wholesalers & Suppliers

Simplify the systems behind inventory distribution.

Connect content, pricing, booking, and financial workflows through a consistent infrastructure foundation. Commercial distribution opportunities are handled through KabuK Exchange where applicable.

Typical topics to discuss

  • Content normalization and mapping
  • Commercial and pricing rules
  • Booking and reservation states
  • Invoicing and reconciliation

Discuss Supplier Infrastructure

Travel Companies

Build on travel-specific infrastructure.

Explore technology services designed around booking lifecycles, commercial rules, partner operations, and integrations—without assuming that every business needs to build each foundation component independently.

Typical topics to discuss

  • Booking lifecycle services
  • Partner access and administration
  • Integration patterns
  • Operational tooling

Working with us

How an engagement typically starts.

An indicative sequence. The actual path depends on your systems, scope, and commercial model.

  1. Share your requirements

    Tell us about your business, systems, and the workflows you want to connect.

  2. Assess fit and scope

    We review which Platform services are relevant and what is available for your use case.

  3. Plan the integration

    Agree on systems, data, responsibilities, and the commercial framework.

  4. Implement in stages

    Start with a defined scope and extend once the foundation is working for you.
