Technology

Core services that work together.

KabuK Platform organizes foundational travel technology into connected service domains. Specific integrations and availability depend on the use case and implementation scope.

Service architecture

Coordinated services, one foundation.

Each domain has a clear responsibility. Together they share integration patterns, data, permissions, and operational visibility.

Platform capabilities

Service domains

Pricing & Availability

Core service

Configure pricing logic, availability workflows, and distribution-related commercial rules.

Covers

  • Pricing rules and rate structures
  • Availability and allotment workflows
  • Channel- and partner-specific commercial rules
  • Rule management for operations teams

Scope note: Pricing is rule-based. Automated or machine-learning price optimization is not part of the current scope.

Booking & Reservations

Core service

Coordinate booking transactions and the reservation lifecycle across connected systems.

Covers

  • Booking transactions
  • Reservation states and history
  • Modifications and cancellations
  • Downstream operational events

Scope note: Support for specific change, cancellation, and refund flows depends on each supplier and integration.

Billing & Payments

Core service

Support invoicing, reconciliation, accounts payable and receivable, and settlement workflows.

Covers

  • Invoicing
  • Reconciliation
  • Accounts payable and receivable
  • Settlement-related workflows

Scope note: KabuK Platform supports financial workflows. It is not a licensed payment processor or escrow service.

Content & Mapping

Core service

Organize accommodation content and normalize supplier, property, and room information.

Covers

  • Property and room content
  • Supplier data normalization
  • Property and room mapping
  • Content quality workflows

Scope note: Mapping quality depends on source data. Mappings are reviewed as part of operations.

Identity & Administration

Core service

Manage partner access and operational administration through defined permissions and tools.

Covers

  • Partner and user access
  • Roles and permissions
  • Administrative tooling
  • Activity records for operations

Scope note: Security practices and any certifications are shared during engagement, not claimed here.

Analytics & Automation

In developmentAI workflows planned

Build operational visibility and explore AI-assisted workflows for repetitive processes.

Covers

  • Operational reporting
  • Transaction and partner visibility
  • Exception monitoring
  • AI-assisted workflows (planned)

Scope note: AI-assisted workflows, including KAOS, are planned and in development. They are not generally available.

Connectivity

Common integration patterns.

KabuK Platform is designed to connect supplier, channel, and operational systems through consistent patterns rather than one-off integrations. PMS, CRS, and channel managers act as connectivity providers; which systems can be connected is confirmed during scoping.

  • Consistent data structures across partners
  • Defined reservation and status events
  • Partner onboarding with explicit responsibilities

Operations

Built to be operated, not just integrated.

Travel transactions continue long after a booking is made. Platform services are designed with operational controls so teams can see state, act on exceptions, and keep records consistent.

  • Visibility of transaction and partner lifecycles
  • Controls for exceptions and manual intervention
  • Consistent records across pricing, booking, and finance

This page describes service areas and design intent. It is not a service-level agreement, a public API specification, or a commitment that any capability is available as a standalone product.
